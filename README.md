# Wiki Stats

Wiki Stats brings a little more magic to your Wikipedia reading. It’s an intelligent sidebar that tracks your progress, remembers where you've been, and suggests new things to explore.

## About

Wiki Stats is a Chrome extension that makes reading Wikipedia even better. It automatically tracks the articles you read and gives you helpful insights. Whether you're doing deep research or just curious, it helps you see your journey and find new topics you'll love.

## Features

### 📊 **Track Your Reading**
- See how many articles you’ve explored.
- Keep a simple history of your reading path.
- Your progress is saved automatically.

### 🗂️ **Smart Sidebar**
- A clean sidebar that stays out of your way.
- Designed to feel like a natural part of Wikipedia.
- Easy to hide when you want to focus.

### 🧭 **Your Path**
- See exactly how you reached your current article.
- Understand how different topics connect.
- Quickly jump back to where you started.

### 💡 **Smart Suggestions**
- Get recommendations based on what you like to read.
- Find related topics you might have missed.
- Keep the learning journey going.

### 🔒 **Private & Secure**
- Everything is stored on your computer.
- No external servers and no tracking.
- Your reading history is for your eyes only.

## Getting Started

1. **Add to Chrome**: Download the extension and add it to your browser.
2. **Start Reading**: Just visit any Wikipedia page.
3. **Explore**: Use the sidebar to see your stats, your path, and new suggestions.

## Privacy

Your privacy is the most important thing. This extension works entirely on your computer and never sends your data anywhere else.
